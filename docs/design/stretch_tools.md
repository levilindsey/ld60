# Stretch tools

Ledger: **LX29** (tweezers) and **LX30** (the soldering iron), not
scheduled. The rest are ideas. Each one is another tool for the rack
([tools.md](tools.md) "Adding a tool").

## Tweezers (LX29)

- Like the board game Operation: lift a tiny thing out of a narrow gap
  without touching the sides.
- Press to close the tips on the thing, drag it out, and release to
  drop it in the tray. Touching a wall buzzes, drops the thing back
  where it was, and costs a small defect.
- The tips tremble a little, more when moving fast.
- Uses: lint on a ball mouse roller, a coin inside a cassette deck, a
  lost spring, a broken gear tooth, the glowing pellet in the atomic
  toaster.

## Soldering iron (LX30)

- **Heat:** hold the tip on a joint until it glows (0.5 to 1 s).
- **Solder:** keep holding, and the solder flows into a shiny cone.
- Too short leaves a dull cold joint that fails at the payoff. Too long
  lifts the pad (a burn decal and a defect). Too much solder bridges to
  the next pad.
- **Desolder:** heat the joint and wiggle the part out (the parts bin's
  wiggle).
- Uses: a capacitor that is soldered in, a loose wire (the connected
  and loose part states).

## Other tool ideas

| Tool | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Pry tool (spudger) | Find the seam and drag along it; clips pop one at a time. Too fast snaps a clip, which then needs glue. |
| Analog multimeter | Drag two probes onto test points and the needle swings. Diagnosis instead of guessing. |
| Pencil | Rewind a cassette by circling the cursor. The eraser cleans cartridge contacts. |
| Ratchet or hex key | Back-and-forth drags with clicks; the hex key needs reseating every quarter turn. |
| Screw extractor or drill | The careful fix for a stripped screw: slower than pliers, with no scratches. Keep it straight. |
| Degausser | Circle it in front of a CRT, then back away slowly to clear the color blotches. |
| Tuning tool | Turn a small trimmer until the static clears, guided by sound and picture. |
| Heat gun and suction cup | Soften the glue (too long melts the plastic), then lift slowly (too fast cracks it). |
| Duct tape | A fast hack for cracks: quick, ugly, lower quality. |
| Rubber band | The classic trick for a stripped Phillips screw: one more try with a better grip. |
