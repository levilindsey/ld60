# Items

Ledger: **LX16** (M1, the first item), **LX23** (M3, the rest of the
jam cut) and **LX35** (more items, not scheduled). Depends on every
tool doc. How an item is built is in [repairables.md](repairables.md).

Items get more outlandish as the game goes on: everyday gadgets first,
then bigger machines, then atomic-age gadgets from a future that never
happened. Real products named below are inspiration only; give them
invented names in the game.

## How to read an entry

- **Ticket:** what the customer says. It hints at the fault.
- **Steps:** in order. Screws open and close each layer.
- **Teaches:** the new thing this item introduces.
- **Payoff:** what happens when it works again. Every item needs one.

## The jam cut (proposed, LX6)

Build in this order and cut from the end.

### 1. Virtual pet egg (the tutorial, M1)

- **Ticket:** "My pet died in 1998."
- **Steps:** 2 flathead screws on the back shell, which lifts off.
  Canned air on 2 dust clumps. Wiggle out the dead coin cell and drop
  in a new one from the parts bin. The shell goes back on and the 2
  screws go back in.
- **Steps to add later:** battery crust on the contacts (the rag), and
  putting the coin cell in the right way up (orientation).
- **Teaches:** layers, the flathead, canned air, the parts bin, the
  outbox.
- **Payoff:** the pet hatches and beeps a little tune.
- **Par:** 60 s.

### 2. Rotary phone (M3)

- **Ticket:** "The dial sticks and it never rings."
- **Steps:** 2 flathead screws on the base plate. The shell lifts off
  (no fasteners). Canned air on the dusty dial. Oil the dial's pivot (it
  squeaks when poked). Hammer the bent bell clapper back into line.
  Shell, base plate, screws.
- **Teaches:** the oil can (the squeak shows it is needed) and the
  hammer.
- **Payoff:** spin the dial and it rings, a big classic bell.
- **Par:** 90 s.

### 3. Walkman (M3)

- **Ticket:** "It plays like a sad robot."
- **Steps:** 4 small Phillips screws on the back. Wipe the tape heads
  with the rag, but not the paper label next to them (a sensitive
  surface). Replace the snapped belt (a plain part swap for the jam; the
  stretchy belt is LX31). Glue the cracked battery door clip. The back
  goes on with its 4 screws.
- **Teaches:** the Phillips gauge, the rag and sensitive surfaces,
  glue.
- **Payoff:** their mixtape plays a short tune.
- **Par:** 120 s.

### 4. CRT TV (M3)

- **Ticket:** "Blotchy, and it flickers till I smack it."
- **Steps:** 6 Phillips screws on the back cover, one of them rusted
  (oil it first). Canned air on heavy dust and a cobweb. Wiggle out the
  bulging capacitor (a yank cracks the board) and fit a new one. Smack
  the case to unstick the relay inside: percussive maintenance with a
  low band, and the glass cracks if hit too hard. The cover goes back on
  with its 6 screws.
- **Teaches:** seized screws, the hammer's jog, the yank risk.
- **Payoff:** the picture locks in, showing the game's title.
- **Par:** 180 s.

### 5. Ray gun (M3, the first atompunk item)

- **Ticket:** "Stopped going pew. Please hurry, the Martians."
- **Steps:** 2 flathead screws and 1 Phillips on the fin cover. Swap the
  cracked power crystal, which glows once seated. Jog the warbler coil
  with the hammer. Glue the cracked fin. Oil the trigger. The cover goes
  back on.
- **Teaches:** that the shop is getting weird.
- **Payoff:** it fires across the shop with a screen flash, and the
  plant on the shelf is gone.
- **Par:** 150 s.

### Bridges: real gadgets that already look atompunk

- **Theremin:** two antennas, one of them bent (realign), and a tube
  swap. It plays a spooky note when fixed.
- **Picture phone** (after AT&T's 1964 Picturephone): a phone with a
  tiny CRT.
- **Atomic energy lab kit** (after the 1950s Gilbert U-238 kit): a
  Geiger counter tube to swap, and "do not lick" on the box.
- **Smoking robot** (after Westinghouse's 1939 Elektro): its voice
  record skips, and it puffs smoke rings when fixed.

### Finale ideas

- **Time machine dial:** its screws turn backwards (loosening tightens
  them), and parts in the bin come from the future.
- **Alien artifact:** no booklet, an unknown fastener, and the tools
  behave a little wrong.
